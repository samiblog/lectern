# Complex layouts

Ten examples of dense, asymmetric layouts, in Korean. See the basic examples first. All pass the check with no issues, and their text can be edited in place.

| No. | Deck | Look for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Dashboard](01-대시보드.md) | 9–12 cells, spanning cells, `fr` and `px`, empty spots |
| 2 | [Magazine](02-잡지-지면.md) | Big picture, three columns, front page, background with a grid |
| 3 | [Process map](03-프로세스-지도.md) | 16 cells, 21 links, two hubs, loops, swimlanes, org chart |
| 4 | [Gantt and calendar](04-간트와-달력.md) | 13-column Gantt, milestones on top, calendar |
| 5 | [Frameworks](05-분석-틀.md) | Quadrants, risk matrix, bubble map, SWOT, RACI, pyramid |
| 6 | [Mixed](06-혼합-구성.md) | Placed cells on a grid, dark grid over a photo |
| 7 | [Restructured dashboard](07-구조를-바꾼-대시보드.md) | No. 1 with a wider grid and merged cells |
| 8 | [Learn by editing](08-고치며-배우는-격자.md) | Editing grids, columns and positions in Structure view |
| 9 | [Many cells](09-칸이-많은-장.md) | 35-cell calendar, 13-cell map, a cell on top |
| 10 | [Same everywhere](10-어디서-봐도-같은-장.md) | 4:3 mixed layouts |
