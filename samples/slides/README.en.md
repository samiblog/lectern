# Slide best practices

Ten model decks, one for each situation. View the finished deck in the slide window, then follow its process document to see how it was built from scratch. The decks and process documents are in Korean.

> [!TIP]
> The installed samples are read-only. To follow along, create a new deck in your own project with **New** (Ctrl+N), or copy this folder and open the copy.

## The shared steps

1. **Decide first.** Who is listening, what they decide, and how many slides.
2. **Ask Claude.** Paste a request into the terminal and point it to the writing guide (`web/help/slides.en.md`).
3. **Write the titles first.** The titles alone should tell the story.
4. **Fill the cells.** Pick a layout that fits the amount of content.
5. **Fix the check.** Paste the **Check** list from the status bar into the terminal, and Claude fixes it.
6. **Polish by hand.** Press F2 to edit in place.
7. **Present and share.** Press Shift+F5 to present, and use **Print / Save as PDF…** from the right-click menu.
