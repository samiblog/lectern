# Contents

> The contents on the right show the current document's headings and elements such as tables and images; click one to go there. Drag a heading to move its section.

## At a glance

| To do | Mouse | Keys |
|---|---|---|
| Show or hide the contents | **Contents** at the right end of the tab bar | **Alt+O** |
| Go to a heading or element | Click its line in the contents | — |
| Hide or show one kind | The buttons above the contents, such as 'Tables 2' and 'Code 5' | — |
| Change the contents width | Drag the border between the document and the contents (double-click for the default width) | — |
| Move a section | Drag a heading in the contents onto another heading | **Ctrl+Z** to undo |

## What appears in the contents

| View | Contents |
|---|---|
| md document · HTML document | Headings, and under each the section's tables, images, charts, boxes, callouts, diagrams, code and math — like 'Table 1 · Price comparison · 6 × 4' |
| Lectern slides | The list of slides — slides to check are marked |
| PDF | Its bookmarks (two levels) — [Viewing PDFs](3.8-pdf.en.md) |
| Code | Classes, functions, methods and separator comments such as `# ---- Drawing ----` — for config files, keys and sections |

To-dos, links and sources are not in the contents — see them in [Elements](3.7-elements.en.md). While an Elements tab is showing, this panel shows the outline of what is collected (documents and the elements under them); while a [laid-out clip box](3.5-highlights-notes-clip.en.md) is showing, the outline of its clips. Kinds you hide with the buttons above stay hidden next time.

## When you click

| Case | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Where you are reading | Marked in the contents |
| Clicking a heading or element | Goes there and outlines it briefly — unfolds the section if it is folded |
| Current line | Moves there |
| Going back | **Alt+←** — to where you were before jumping from the contents |
| In edit mode | The contents mark the section with the cursor (if the cursor is off screen, the section at the top of the screen); clicking takes the editor to that line |

## Moving sections

This works in md documents you can edit. It does not work in view-only projects (the samples), in the full editor, or in Lectern slides viewed as slides.

| Drop on | Result |
|---|---|
| Another heading | Moves the section, with its body and subsections, in front of that heading |
| The lower part of the last heading | Moves it to the end of the document |
| Heading level | Unchanged |
| Undo | **Ctrl+Z** |

## Terminal and contents

| Terminal position | Contents |
|---|---|
| Right | The terminal takes the contents' place — the **Contents** button shows both together |
| Below the document (when the window is tall) | The contents stay as they are |
