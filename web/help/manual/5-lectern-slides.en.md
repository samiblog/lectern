# Lectern slides

> View an md document with `marp: true` in its front matter as slides with a different layout on each, write on them in place, present them, and export them to pptx.

Lectern tells three things apart — **md document** (ordinary Markdown), **Lectern slides** (md with `marp: true` — an orange icon in the left list) and **pptx** (a PowerPoint file).

| Step | What to do | Keys | Documents in this chapter |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | In New document, pick a template to make the first slide | **Ctrl+N** | [Writing slides](5.1-writing-slides.en.md) · [Templates](5.3-templates.en.md) |
| 2 | Replace the placeholder text with your content, and go on by picking the next slide's template | **F2** · **Ctrl+M** | [Writing slides](5.1-writing-slides.en.md) |
| 3 | Check for overflow and mistakes, then present | **Shift+F5** | [Presenting and checking](5.2-presenting-and-checking.en.md) |
| 4 | Pick a pptx style and export to pptx, or to a presentation html | **Convert to pptx** · **Convert to html** in the status bar | [pptx and html presentations](6.4-pptx-and-html-presentations.en.md) |

The Example guide (**Shift+F1**) has Lectern slides samples in the order basics, by situation and complex layouts — the samples are view only, so follow along in your own project.
